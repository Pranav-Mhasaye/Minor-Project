    Text (6 paragraphs):
      • Problem Statement Reference: Smart India Hackathon SIH 26124 | Organization: Bharat Electronics Limited (BEL)
      • • 1. Delayed Road Hazard Remediation & High Survey Costs: Potholes, damaged dividers, and waterlogging persist for weeks without inspection, causing fatal accidents and vehicle damage. Inspection currently relies on slow citizen complaints or costly dedicated survey vehicles ($$$).
      • • 2. Severe Spatial Blind Spots of Static CCTVs: Fixed surveillance cameras cover <3% of the municipal road network; scaling static cameras across entire cities costs tens of crores of rupees and leaves vast coverage gaps on arterial and suburban streets.
      • • 3. Cellular Bandwidth & Cloud Saturation Bottleneck: Streaming raw 1080p dashcam video from 500 transit buses would demand ~1.5 Gbps continuous uplink, collapsing 4G/5G cellular towers and creating huge recurring cloud egress and storage bills.
      • • 4. Multi-Bus Spatial Duplication & False Positive Clutter: Multiple buses traveling the same transit corridor report identical defects dozens of times daily. Naive systems flood municipal databases with duplicate entries and overwhelm maintenance work crews.
      • • The Proposed Paradigm: Decentralized edge-AI processing on transit fleets transmitting lightweight telemetry (<5 KB JSON) with event-driven incident recording and spatio-temporal deduplication in the cloud.

      • Primary Technical Objectives:
      • • Objective 1: Lightweight Edge-AI Vision Pipeline: Develop and calibrate an INT8-quantized YOLO11n computer vision detector capable of real-time road hazard detection (potholes, cracks, waterlogging) at ≥25 FPS on low-power edge compute.
      • • Objective 2: Persistent Multi-Object Tracking (ByteTrack): Implement ByteTrack Kalman-filter multi-object tracking to assign consistent track IDs across consecutive video frames, computing defect surface area and eliminating intra-video double counting.
      • • Objective 3: Circular RAM Buffer & Kinematic-Triggered ANPR: Maintain a rolling 15-second uncompressed video buffer in edge RAM, triggered by kinematic/trajectory anomalies for automated high-res ANPR and incident clip capture.
      • • Objective 4: Centralized GIS Dashboard & Spatial Deduplication: Build an interactive Web GIS dashboard with Haversine / ST-DBSCAN spatio-temporal clustering to consolidate repeat fleet observations into verified municipal repair tickets.
      • Scope Division Across Project Phases:
      • • Minor Project (Review II / Phase 1): Core edge-AI detection pipeline, ByteTrack persistent tracking, GPS sentence binding, and interactive Leaflet GIS dashboard prototype.
      • • Major Project (Phase 2): Physical edge hardware deployment on Raspberry Pi 5 + Hailo-8 NPU, vehicle CAN-bus integration, and direct municipal PWD API integration.

--- Shape: 'Content Placeholder 2' (PLACEHOLDER (14)) ---
    Left=0.80", Top=1.35", Width=11.73", Height=5.30"
    Text (10 paragraphs):
      • Method 1: Edge Computer Vision & ByteTrack Multi-Object Association
      • • Model Pipeline: YOLO11n fine-tuned on Indian road datasets (RDD2022 + Roboflow) and quantized to INT8 ONNX (≥25 FPS inference).
      • • Tracking Algorithm: ByteTrack Kalman filter state estimation [u, v, s, r, u', v', s']^T with two-stage IoU association. Matches high- and low-confidence detections to maintain persistent defect IDs and compute surface area without duplicate counting.
      • Method 2: Kinematic-Triggered RAM Ring Buffer & Laplacian Sharpness ANPR
      • • Circular Buffer: 15-second FIFO circular queue in edge RAM preserving pre- and post-incident frames with zero flash drive wear.
      • • Trigger Logic: Deceleration anomaly (>0.4g) or trajectory deviation locks incident video window.
      • • Sharpness Score: Laplacian variance Var(∇² I_gray) selects the sharpest vehicle crop for OCR license plate extraction, eliminating motion blur.
      • Method 3: Spatio-Temporal Deduplication & Asymptotic Bayesian Verification
      • • Haversine Distance Clustering: Observations within d ≤ 3.5 m update existing records: d = 2R · arcsin(√(sin²(Δφ/2) + cos φ1 · cos φ2 · sin²(Δλ/2))).
      • • Bayesian Confidence Accumulation: Formula P(Verified) = 1 - ∏(1 - c_i) — three independent fleet passes with c ≥ 0.80 achieve >99.2% verification confidence, filtering out spurious false positives.
