## 3. Cross-Paper Synthesis & Statistical Breakdown

### A. Publication Year Distribution
- **2022:** 2 papers (9.5%)
- **2023:** 1 paper (4.8%)
- **2024:** 5 papers (23.8%)
- **2025:** 10 papers (47.6%)
- **2026:** 3 papers (14.3%)
- *Observation:* Over **85% of all papers** were published within the last 24 months, confirming that mobile urban sensing and edge AI for transportation is an active, frontier research topic.

### B. Core Deep Learning Architectures
- **YOLO Family (v5, v8, v10, v11):** 57.1% (Dominant choice due to high inference FPS and low memory footprint).
- **Instance Segmentation (YOLO-Seg, POT-YOLO):** 19.0% (Used for polygon defect boundaries).
- **Multi-Object Tracking (ByteTrack, DeepSORT):** 14.3% (Used for persistent defect and vehicle tracking).
- **ANPR & OCR Pipelines (STN, EasyOCR, Tesseract):** 19.0% (Used for license plate extraction).
- **Time-Series / IMU Models (1D-CNN, LSTM):** 9.5% (Used for kinematic rash-driving detection).

### C. Major Research Gaps Identified
1. **Model Siloing:** Previous literature treats pothole detection, traffic flow, and ANPR as mutually exclusive systems.
2. **Lack of Spatial Deduplication:** Existing systems record redundant counts every time a vehicle traverses the same physical defect.
3. **Bandwidth Saturation:** Failure to decouple continuous low-compute telemetry from event-triggered high-resolution forensic video.
